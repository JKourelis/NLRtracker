# NLR signature set: roles, overlaps, and classification rules

Three data tables generated by [`../module/build_signature_table.py`](../module/build_signature_table.py) define NLRtracker's biological logic:

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| [`../module/nlr_signatures.tsv`](../module/nlr_signatures.tsv) | 231 curated signatures with InterPro metadata, domain class, subclass evidence, and provenance |
| [`../module/nlr_classes.tsv`](../module/nlr_classes.tsv) | 41 domain classes with region mapping, merge group, precedence, simplified code, colour, and shape |
| [`../module/nlr_subclass_rules.tsv`](../module/nlr_subclass_rules.tsv) | 1 multi-signature rule (`ccg10_gated`) |

Current reference release: **InterPro `111.0`** (`Pfam 39.0`), indexed offline in `module/InterPro_111.0.list` (`72,944` InterPro entries) from InterProScan `entries.json`.

Evidence base for curated entries:

- **RefPlantNLR:** 481 experimentally validated plant NLRs with published subclass assignments (PLoS Biology `pbio.3001124.s013`, Table S1; 481 of 481 identifiers matched).
- ***Nicotiana tabacum* proteome:** 75,127 proteins evaluated where 481 reference NLRs were too narrow.

## 1. Region roles versus subclass roles

Each signature carries one **region** role and, independently, an optional **subclass** role.

- A **region** role assigns sequence coordinates to an architectural block (`TIR`, `CC`, `RPW8`, `R1`, `NB-ARC`, `PLOOP`, `LRR`, `C-JID`, `MLKL`, or `ID`) for extraction and plotting.
- A **subclass** role informs `Subclass (putative)` without creating a separate N-terminal region.

### Winged-helix signatures sit inside the NB-ARC

`PF23559` (`WHD_DRP`) and `PF23282` (`WHD_ROQ1`) provide strong lineage discrimination across RefPlantNLR:

| Signature | CC-NLR | TIR-NLR | CCG10-NLR | CCR-NLR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `PF23559` | 343 / 347 | 0 / 105 | 8 / 19 | 0 / 10 |
| `PF23282` | 0 / 347 | 99 / 105 | 0 / 19 | 0 / 10 |

Both domains belong to the C-terminal winged-helix unit inside the NB-ARC rather than an N-terminal domain. Eight of 19 RefPlantNLR `CCG10-NLR` proteins carry `PF23559`, while zero of 19 carry any true CC signature (`PF18052`, `cd14798`, or `G3DSA:1.20.5.4130`). Assigning `PF23559` and `PF23282` to `Winged-helix` (`region = NB-ARC`, `drop_after = yes`) preserves clean N-terminal FASTA boundaries while raising CC-lineage sensitivity from 85% (`PF18052` alone) to 99%. Similarly, `G3DSA:1.10.10.10` hits 333/347 CC-NLRs and `SSF46785` hits 54/105 TIR-NLRs as NB-ARC extension signatures.

### Boundary arbitration order

1. Build `NB-ARC` regions (and merge overlapping `PLOOP`, `Winged-helix`, and NB-ARC motif runs).
2. Truncate `TIR`, `CC`, `RPW8`, and `R1` to end before the nearest overlapping `NB-ARC` or `PLOOP`.
3. Truncate `LRR` to start after the `NB-ARC` or `PLOOP`.
4. Truncate `C-JID` to start after `LRR`.
5. Subtract all canonical regions from `ID` intervals, splitting an integrated domain if needed.

Regions trimmed below zero length are dropped and logged in `raw/logs/dropped_regions.tsv`.

### Merge groups

| Group | Precedence order | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| `nbarc` | `NBARC` (40) > `NACHT` (30) > `PLOOP` (20) > `nbarc-motif` / `Winged-helix` / `nacht-winged-helix` (10) | Overlapping `PLOOP` merges into `NBARC`; extension classes (`drop_after`) are stripped afterwards |
| `rpw8` | `RPW8` (40) > `MLKL` (30) > `CblN` (20) | Preserves `RPW8` priority over overlapping `MLKL` or `CblN` |
| `id` | Specific IDs (40) > `OTHER` / `PLOOP-OTHER` (20) > super-structure repeats (10) | Specific IDs merge against `OTHER` pairwise so unrelated IDs stay separated |

### Signature caveats

- **`PF23598` (`LRR_14`) is not strictly NLR-specific:** Across RefPlantNLR it shows 87% CC-NLR purity (259 CC-NLRs, 25 TIR-NLRs, 3 CCR-NLRs), and proteome testing shows it also matches cell-surface LRR receptor kinases and proteins such as `FLS2`. It serves as LRR region and extraction evidence only, with no subclass role. By contrast, `PF25019`, `PF23247`, and `PF23286` are NLR-specific within the curated receptor set.
- **`PF25895`:** Absent from RefPlantNLR but confirmed six times across the *N. tabacum* proteome as a plant disease resistance winged-helix domain.
- **`SSF47668` and `PF06760` (`MLKL`):** `SSF47668` shares `IPR036537` with `G3DSA:1.20.930.20` and targets the mammalian-biased four-helix bundle; plant hits route through `cd21037` (38 in *N. tabacum*) and `G3DSA:1.20.930.20` (36). In InterPro `111.0`, `PF06760` renamed from `DUF1221` to `F22L4_1` (`F22L4.1-like N-terminal domain`).

## 4. Gated `CCG10-NLR` classification

None of the 19 `CCG10-NLR` members in RefPlantNLR carry a detectable `CC` domain signature (`PF18052`, `cd14798`, or `G3DSA:1.20.5.4130`), and `G3DSA:1.20.5.4220` does not exist in InterPro `108.0` through `111.0`. `PF23247` (`LRR_RPS2`) alone gives 52% precision (11 true positives, 10 CC-NLR false positives), whereas gating out true `CC` domains achieves 100% precision:

| Rule | Selected | True CCG10-NLR | False positives | Precision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `PF23247` alone | 21 | 11 / 19 | 10 | 52% |
| `PF23247` and not (`PF18052` or `cd14798`) | 11 | 11 / 19 | 0 | 100% |

This rule runs after diagnostic `TIR` and `RPW8` calls and before generic `CC` routes, emitting `CCG10-NLR (probable)` (`subclass_support = probable`). The remaining 8 `CCG10-NLR` proteins carry `PF23559` without `PF23247` and report `CC-NLR or CCG10-NLR` (`lineage` support).

## 5. InterPro `111.0` (`Pfam 39.0`) audit

All `231` curated entries (`218` InterPro-mapped accessions, `12` MEME motifs, and `CJID`) remain valid in InterPro `111.0` with **0 withdrawn**. Four entries changed metadata in `111.0`:

- `PF06760`: short name `DUF1221` changed to `F22L4_1` (`F22L4.1-like N-terminal domain`).
- `PF06985`: description updated to `Heterokaryon incompatibility protein (HET) domain`.
- `PF09976`: description updated to `SecYEG/CpoB, TPR domain`.
- `G3DSA:3.40.395.10`: integrated into `IPR065533` (`Adenoviral Proteinase; Chain A`).

### Pfam `39.0` `PF00931.30` shrinkage and `TN2`

In Pfam `39.0`, `PF00931.30` (`NB-ARC`) shortened from `175` aa to `152` aa. Because `classify()` determines `Status` from pre-HMM InterPro domain annotations, `TN2` (`RefPlantNLR` TIR-NLR) loses its `PF00931` hit under `111.0` and moves from `Status = NLR` to `Status = degenerate NLR`. Its `Subclass (putative)` (`TIR-NLR`, `subclass_support = domain`) and its `NB-ARC` region (`168–374` from `NB-ARC_region.hmm`) remain unchanged.

### Name resolution fallbacks

Nine signatures carry neither a signature description nor an InterPro entry description in InterProScan `6` TSVs and retain local fallback names in `module/R/00_config.R` (`NAME_FALLBACK`): `G3DSA:1.20.5.4130`, `G3DSA:1.10.533.20`, `G3DSA:3.30.70.100`, `G3DSA:3.10.20.370`, `G3DSA:1.10.340.70`, `cd00200`, `cd02947`, `cd02989`, and `PR00364`.

Scored against the 481 published RefPlantNLR assignments (`GR` mapped to `CCR-NLR`, while bare `RPW8` denotes NB-ARC-less NLR-associated proteins):

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `domain` | 443 | 443 | 0 | 0 |
| `lineage` | 23 | 3 | 20 | 0 |
| `probable` | 11 | 11 | 0 | 0 |
| `motif` | 2 | 0 | 2 | 0 |
| `supporting` | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 |
| *(empty)* | 1 | 0 | 0 | 1 |
| **Total** | **481** | **457 (95.0%)** | **23 (4.8%)** | **1 (0.2%)** |

Zero wrong calls occur in any tier backed by subclass evidence. The single error (`SpNBS-LRR`) lacks any subclass marker and is reported honestly as `UNDETERMINED`.

## 7. Known gaps

- **CCG10-NLR sensitivity:** `11 / 19` are called `CCG10-NLR (probable)`; the other `8` report `CC-NLR or CCG10-NLR`.
- **MLKL validation:** RefPlantNLR contains NLRs rather than MLKLs; `cd21037` rests on 38 proteome observations.
- **`PF25895` sample size:** Curated from 6 proteome hits.
- **`CblN` validation:** Only one hit exists in RefPlantNLR.
